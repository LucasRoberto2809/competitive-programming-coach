# Rubric clarification — preregistered before clean baseline

The first draft left “core competency” undefined. For evaluation, the first nine competencies in experiment-config.md are core; independent transfer is scored separately and must reach at least 2 without step-by-step guidance.

Score coach/session evidence separately where applicable:
- Implementation competency: student-authored code score and student explanation of implementation score are recorded separately. Completion requires code score 3.
- Correctness reasoning: student’s own correctness explanation must score 3 for completion.
- Other core competencies: each must score at least 2.
- Transfer: student must attempt the unseen transfer problem without stepwise coaching and score at least 2.

A concept introduced by the coach is not demonstrated until the student explains or applies it. Do not award a score from confidence alone. Evaluator must cite exact transcript evidence and distinguish direct observations from likely causes.
